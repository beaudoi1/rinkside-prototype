Littleton Hockey Association
Youth hockey association · Not affiliated with any single rink
✓ USA Hockey Affiliate 6 teams
Littleton Hockey Association organizes youth hockey — team registration, practices, games, and tournaments — across the Littleton area. LHA doesn't own ice; games and practices are held at whichever rinks are booked for a given season, which can vary team to team and even week to week. Registration requires current USA Hockey membership.
